## P1B-037 Selection Boundary

**Before:** An integrated pneumatic and electrical solution may be considered when the required air passages and electrical circuits exceed the capacity of a pneumatic-only rotary union.

**After:** A separate pneumatic rotary union and slip ring may be suitable when the pneumatic and electrical functions can be packaged independently and the available installation space supports two components. An integrated pneumatic-electrical solution may be considered when a single compact assembly, coordinated mounting, or a higher combined channel density is required. Final selection depends on the pneumatic passage count, bore requirements, electrical circuits, signal type, speed, installation envelope, and service access.

Result: the independent and integrated solution boundaries are both present without claiming that either is universally better or cheaper.

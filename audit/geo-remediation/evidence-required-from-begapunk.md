## Handling Rules

- Store source documents outside the public web root and record document owner, issue date, expiry date, and applicable page/model.
- Do not upload customer-confidential files, personal data, passwords, or authorization codes into the repository.
- Approved public wording should cite the evidence record ID and its scope; unsupported values should be removed or qualified only after manual approval.

Air Rotary Union for CNC Pneumatic Clamping Fixtures

CNC rotary fixtures and indexing tables need reliable compressed air transfer for clamping, unclamping, pressure holding, and blow-off. Begapunk helps match passage count, flange layout, bore size, and RPM for machining automation.

Application Fit

Keep clamping pressure stable while the fixture rotates

Who this is for: CNC machine tool builders, fixture designers, and manufacturing engineers integrating pneumatic clamping into rotary tables and machining centers.

When a CNC fixture rotates, its compressed-air lines must not twist or pull on the actuator. A pneumatic rotary union provides sealed rotating passages for compressed air between the stationary air-supply side and the rotating fixture.

The most common design mistake is choosing by outside diameter only. Engineers should confirm pressure, flow, passage count, rotational speed, mounting face, and how the hose load will be supported.

Typical CNC Pneumatic Clamping Rotary Union Specs

  • Pressure: 0.4–0.8 MPa (clamping); confirm the continuous-duty limit for the selected model against the current product page, approved drawing, medium, temperature, and machining cycle
  • RPM: <200 (most fixtures) / up to 500 (high-speed rotary tables)
  • Passages: 1–4 (clamp + unclamp + blow-off + coolant mist)
  • Media: dry air + water-soluble coolant — seal must resist both
  • Mounting: flange mount preferred for fixture plate integration
  • Bore: 6–10 mm for standard clamps; 16–30 mm for large fixture plates
  • Seal: Glyd Ring (PTFE composite) recommended for coolant compatibility
Engineering Points

CNC Fixture Selection Checklist

Pressure holding

Clamping pressure must remain stable during indexing and machining. Leakage can affect part positioning and process repeatability.

Passage separation

  • Clamp
  • Unclamp
  • Blow-off
  • Sensor or auxiliary air

Mechanical interface

Check flange bolt pattern, shaft bore, anti-rotation support, and whether the rotary joint is exposed to chips or coolant mist.

FAQ

CNC Fixture Questions

Can a rotary union hold clamping pressure during machining?

Yes, if the model is correctly selected for pressure, seal condition, and hose loading. The fixture circuit should also include suitable pressure control.

Can Begapunk match a fixture drawing?

Yes. For CNC fixtures, drawings are strongly recommended because mounting dimensions and port location usually decide the final model.

Air Rotary Union for Bottle Filling and Capping Machines

Rotary filling and capping equipment requires stable pneumatic transfer for grippers, lifting cylinders, capping heads, blow-off, and torque-related functions. Begapunk helps choose compact rotary joints for repeated production cycles.

Application Fit

Pneumatic transfer for rotating filling and capping heads

In bottle handling equipment, several pneumatic actions may happen while the machine is rotating: bottle positioning, cap pickup, capping pressure, gripper open-close, and blow-off. A rotary union keeps these lines connected without twisting hoses.

For food, beverage, and chemical packaging lines, the air rotary union must also support clean routing, quick maintenance, and predictable leakage behavior.

Typical requirementCompact 1P, 2P, or 3P pneumatic rotary joint for capping heads and filling carousels.
Engineering Points

Selection Checklist

Head count and passages

Confirm how many rotating functions require independent air lines. A single shared line may not be enough for modern capping heads.

Product environment

Cleaning routines, humidity, and splash exposure can influence material and seal choices.

Maintenance access

Filling machines often need fast replacement during line stoppage. Standardized mounting and drawing review help reduce downtime.

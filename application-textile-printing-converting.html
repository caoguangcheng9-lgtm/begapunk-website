Rotary Union for Printing Machinery and Converting Equipment

Printing machines, presses, laminators, textile winders, and converting lines use rotary unions to transfer air, vacuum, water, or coolant into rotating rollers and drums without twisting fixed hoses.

Application Fit

How printing machinery uses rotary unions

Who this is for: Printing machinery builders, printing press manufacturers, textile machinery OEMs, and converting equipment designers specifying rotary transfer for rollers and winding systems.

A rotary union for printing machinery provides a sealed path between the stationary supply and a rotating roller or drum. Typical functions include pneumatic actuation, air blow-off, vacuum holding, and water or coolant transfer for temperature control.

Start the selection with six inputs: every medium, operating pressure, roller RPM, passage count, shaft bore, and mounting envelope. Then account for lint, paper dust, ink, or adhesive contamination that may affect seal protection and maintenance access.

If air, vacuum, and liquid must remain isolated, assign independent passages rather than combining circuits. Compare the machine drawing with the rotary union interface, port direction, and anti-rotation arrangement before choosing a standard model or requesting a custom layout.

Typical requirementAir, vacuum, water, or coolant transfer for rotating rollers, winding shafts, printing drums, and converting equipment.

Typical Textile & Printing Rotary Union Specs

  • Pressure: 0.2–0.6 MPa (blow-off) / -0.04 to -0.08 MPa (vacuum) / 0.1–0.4 MPa (water)
  • RPM: 100–500 (roller speed) — up to 2,000 for high-speed winding
  • Passages: 2–4 (air + vacuum + water/coolant)
  • Media: dry air, vacuum, water, coolant — specify all media when inquiring
  • Mounting: flange or through-bore for roller shaft integration
  • Bore: 10–30 mm (roller shafts) — through-bore must match shaft diameter
  • Seal: PTFE skeleton oil seal for >500 RPM roller applications
  • Environment: lint, paper dust, ink, adhesive — specify contamination type

FAQ

Printing Machinery Rotary Union Questions

Are rotary unions used in printing and converting equipment?

Yes. They are used when air, vacuum, water, coolant, or other media must pass into rotating rollers or drums.

Can one rotary union handle both air and water?

It can be possible with separated passages, but media, pressure, speed, and seal requirements must be confirmed.

How do I select a rotary union for printing machinery?

Confirm every medium, operating pressure, roller RPM, passage count, shaft bore, mounting space, and contamination source. Use separate passages for media that must not mix, then verify the interface against the machine drawing before selection.

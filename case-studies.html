Pneumatic Rotary Union Installation on a Laser Tube Cutting Rear Chuck

Application: laser tube cutting machine rear-chuck assembly

Visible Installation Evidence

Two workshop photographs show pneumatic rotary unions installed near rear-chuck rotating structures. The visible evidence includes the chuck structure, mounting area, flange arrangement and pneumatic fittings. The two photographs belong to the same application category; they are not presented as two views of the same machine.

The rotary union provides a stationary-to-rotating connection for the rear chuck's compressed-air circuits. Passage assignment and operating conditions must be confirmed from the machine documentation.

Begapunk Models Used in This Application

BP-3P-0004 and BP-2P-08-0001 are used in pneumatic rear-chuck applications on laser tube cutting machines. The product shown in each photograph has not been individually identified as either model.

Engineering Confirmation Items

Before selecting or ordering a rotary union for a similar rear-chuck integration, provide the machine and chuck information needed for a written engineering review:

  • Required pneumatic circuits
  • Normal and peak working pressure
  • Maximum and continuous rotational speed
  • Mounting interface and chuck drawing
  • Available installation envelope
  • Pneumatic hose direction and fitting clearance
  • Maintenance access
  • Exact model to be ordered

Confirm the ordered model, passage count, port functions, operating conditions and mounting interface from the machine documentation and approved drawing. The confirmed scope for the two standard models is the rear chuck's compressed-air circuits; process or assist-gas transfer is not included.

BP-3P-S06-0001 in a Sensor-Monitored Pneumatic Chuck

Application: pneumatic actuation and external sensor-signal transfer at a rotating chuck

Confirmed Customer Configuration

In this customer design, three independent compressed-air circuits are assigned to clamping, unclamping and blow-off. The electrical slip ring transfers external sensor signals that the machine controller may use for workpiece-presence or clamped-position confirmation. Detection remains the responsibility of the external sensors and control system.

Engineering Selection Example

Example 1: Custom Review for Corrosive Outdoor Duty

Selection objective: control corrosion, contamination, and maintenance risk

Engineering Requirement

Salt, humidity, temperature cycling, and difficult maintenance access can change the required housing finish, shaft material, bearing protection, seal compound, and test plan. A standard indoor pneumatic model should not be assumed suitable for offshore service.

Input requiredWhy it matters
Media and concentrationDetermines seal and metal compatibility.
Pressure, RPM, and duty cycleDefines the combined pressure-speed operating point.
Temperature rangeAffects elastomer selection and dimensional clearances.
Corrosion or test standardDefines coating, material, and validation requirements.
Maintenance intervalInfluences bearing protection and serviceability.

Candidate Direction

BP-2P-130-0001 is a heavy-duty catalog reference, not an automatic offshore solution. Any corrosive or safety-critical application requires drawing review, material confirmation, and an agreed validation plan.

Example 2: CAD Fit Check Before CNC Installation

Selection objective: verify bore, flange, ports, and surrounding clearance

Engineering Requirement

Even when pressure and RPM are suitable, installation can fail because of bolt-pattern mismatch, insufficient hollow-bore clearance, hose interference, or rigid piping loads. A CAD fit check reduces dimensional risk before production.

CheckRequired confirmation
Hollow boreClearance around the shaft, drawbar, cable, or tooling.
EnvelopeBody diameter, total length, and service access.
MountingBolt circle, thread specification, depth, and locating feature.
Ports and hosesFitting size, bend radius, and freedom from external load.
Operating pointMedia, normal pressure, peak pressure, RPM, and duty cycle.

Candidate Product

BP-2P-30-0001 is a hollow-bore catalog reference. Download or request the current drawing and confirm all interfaces and operating conditions in the machine assembly before ordering.

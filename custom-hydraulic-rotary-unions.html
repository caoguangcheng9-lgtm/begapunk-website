Begapunk manufactures custom hydraulic-oil rotary unions (rotary joints). Typical layout is 2 passages; custom work goes up to 12. We evaluate and manufacture up to 30 MPa (300 bar / ≈ 4350 psi). That figure is a custom ceiling, not a stock catalog rating and not a conversion of the pneumatic catalog.

Inquiry review

How we review a hydraulic inquiry

Engineer to engineer. Start with operating pressure and speed; we will say what else is needed.

  1. 1

    Send pressure and speed

    Operating pressure in bar and psi or MPa, plus speed (RPM or indexed / oscillating).

  2. 2

    Seal stack and passages

    We size the carbon-fiber Glyd ring (PTFE slide ring) stack and the passage count: 2 typical, custom up to 12.

  3. 3

    Ports, envelope, feasibility

    We confirm ports, envelope, and whether the duty is feasible to manufacture.

  4. 4

    MOQ 1, within 30 days

    One piece is enough to start. Custom hydraulic is completed within 30 calendar days after payment. It is not the catalog pneumatic ≈20-day item.

Two offers

Two different offers

Do not treat the drawing-listed 1 MPa catalog model as the excavator 30 MPa custom union.

Drawing-listed oil/water: BP-1P-0003

BP-1P-0003 is the published catalog model whose drawing lists air, oil, and water at its published 1 MPa / 500 RPM. Use that product page for published values. We do not restate a hydraulic catalog bar rating here, and this is not the excavator 30 MPa union.

High-pressure custom hydraulic

Hydraulic oil. Carbon-fiber Glyd ring (PTFE slide ring). Evaluate and manufacture up to 30 MPa (300 bar / ≈ 4350 psi). Typical 2 passages, custom up to 12. MOQ 1 piece. This is a custom ceiling, not a stock rating on every unit.

FAQ

Hydraulic rotary union questions

Can you make hydraulic rotary unions?

Yes. Begapunk manufactures custom hydraulic-oil rotary unions. Typical layout is 2 passages; custom work goes up to 12. We evaluate and manufacture up to 30 MPa (300 bar / ≈ 4350 psi). That is a custom ceiling, not a stock rating on every unit.

Is this the air catalog?

No. The 16 published catalog models are almost all compressed air with PTFE/O-ring seals. Only the BP-1P-0003 drawing lists air/oil/water at its published 1 MPa / 500 RPM. That is a different offer from the 30 MPa custom hydraulic path.

What seal do you use for this custom hydraulic work?

A carbon-fiber Glyd ring (PTFE slide ring) on this custom hydraulic work.

What is the MOQ and lead time?

MOQ is 1 piece. Custom hydraulic is completed within 30 calendar days after payment. Catalog pneumatic remains about 20 days; custom hydraulic is not that 20-day item.

What do you need first?

Operating pressure (bar and psi or MPa) and speed (RPM or indexed / oscillating). A duty sheet or sketch is enough to start.

Do you only want certain industries?

No. We want every hydraulic inquiry type: excavators, log and timber grapples, marine reel and coil-handling equipment, and other hydraulic duties. Send the duty.

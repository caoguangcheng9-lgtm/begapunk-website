FAQ

Hydraulic Rotary Union Questions

Can you make a hydraulic rotary union?

Yes. Begapunk evaluates and manufactures custom hydraulic, oil, water, and coolant rotary unions (rotary joints) for OEM machinery. Send the medium, pressure, and motion so seals, materials, and layout can be reviewed. This is an engineering evaluation, not an off-the-shelf catalog pick.

Are catalog models hydraulic?

No. Published catalog models are primarily pneumatic (compressed air). Do not use air-catalog MPa or RPM figures for hydraulic service. BP-1P-0003 is the published catalog model whose drawing lists air, oil, and water; see the BP-1P-0003 product page for published values. We do not restate a hydraulic bar rating here.

What information do you need for a quote?

Medium (hydraulic oil grade, water, or coolant), pressure in bar and psi, RPM or indexed motion, passage count, port threads, mounting, envelope, and duty. Incomplete details are fine for the first inquiry.

How is a hydraulic rotary union different from a pneumatic rotary union?

Pneumatic catalog units are sized and sealed for compressed air. Hydraulic oil, water, and coolant change seal selection, leakage criteria, and often the housing and passage design. High-pressure hydraulic clamping and mobile equipment are custom engineering only.

What is the lead time?

Catalog pneumatic models are typically about 20 calendar days. Custom hydraulic, oil, water, or coolant units are quoted after engineering review; they are not the catalog 20-day item.

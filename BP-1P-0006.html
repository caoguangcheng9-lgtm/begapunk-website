Installation Guide

1

Verify Thread Compatibility

Confirm your equipment has G1/8 (BSPP) female threads or appropriate adapters. The BP-1P-0006 uses G1/8 male threads with 4× M4 mounting holes on both rotor and stator sides. Download the 2D drawing for exact dimensions.

2

Apply Thread Sealant

Apply PTFE tape or liquid thread sealant to the G1/8 male threads. Hand-tighten first, then tighten to 8–12 N·m with a wrench. Do not overtighten to avoid thread damage.

3

Secure Anti-Rotation

Use the 4× M4 mounting holes to secure the rotary joint body to a fixed bracket. Never let the body rotate freely with the shaft. Torque M4 bolts to 2–3 N·m.

4

Connect Supply Lines

Use flexible hoses to avoid side-loading the rotary joint. Install a filter (40 micron or finer) upstream to protect the seal.

5

Run-In Procedure

Run at low pressure (0.2 MPa) and low speed for 5 minutes without load. Check for leaks, then gradually increase to operating conditions.

Air Rotary Union for Packaging Machinery

Packaging machines need compact, repeatable pneumatic transfer for rotary sealing heads, indexing tables, vacuum stations, grippers, and filling mechanisms. Begapunk supports standard and custom air rotary unions for OEM packaging equipment.

Application Fit

Stable air transfer for high-repeat packaging motion

Packaging machinery usually runs many cycles per shift, so the rotary joint must keep leakage low while tolerating repeated indexing, short dwell times, and compact installation space.

Common functions include pneumatic clamping, vacuum pickup, bag or pouch opening, rotary sealing, blow-off, and air cylinder actuation. Choosing the right passage count prevents control signals from interfering with one another.

Typical requirementCompact pneumatic rotary joint with low leakage, reliable sealing, and simple replacement.
Engineering Points

What to Confirm Before Selection

Cycle and duty

Confirm rotation style, indexing frequency, and whether the machine runs intermittently or 24/7.

Air functions

  • Vacuum pickup
  • Sealing head actuation
  • Blow-off and cleaning air
  • Pneumatic gripper control

Installation space

Many packaging stations have limited envelope space, so body diameter, port direction, and hose routing should be checked early.

FAQ

Packaging Machinery Questions

Can the same rotary joint handle vacuum and compressed air?

Yes, when passages are separated and the seal design is confirmed for the pressure and vacuum level.

Do packaging machines usually need custom models?

Many machines can use standard 1P, 2P, or 3P models. Custom design is useful when space, port direction, or mounting pattern is fixed.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 11, 2026
Last Updated: July 31, 2026

Begapunk Precision Rotary Joint Manufacturer ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or submit an inquiry.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Inquiry and contact information: Name, email address, company name, country, product interest, application details, technical requirements, inquiry type, and source-page information that you submit through the quote request form.
  • Technical information: IP address and request metadata that may be processed by the web server for delivery, security, and logging. The inquiry handler derives a one-way hash from the request IP address and keeps recent attempt timestamps in server temporary storage for a 15-minute rate-limit window.
  • Business and technical information: Technical requirements and optional drawing files in the formats accepted by the form (PDF, STEP/STP, IGES/IGS, DWG, DXF, JPG/JPEG, or PNG), up to 10 MB.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide quotes
  • Process and fulfill orders
  • Verify product fit using uploaded technical drawings
  • Improve our website and product offerings
  • Send the inquiry to our configured sales mailbox through authenticated SMTP
  • Prevent abuse and protect the availability of the inquiry service
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate and improve our website. Below is a breakdown of the cookies we use, their purpose, and how you can control them.

3.1 Essential Local Storage (Always Active)

The consent manager uses browser local storage rather than an essential cookie to remember your analytics choice. This record does not directly identify you and is not sent to our server with normal page requests.

  • Consent state — Stored under begapunk_cookie_consent in localStorage, including the choice, banner version, and timestamp. The site treats the record as expired after 365 days.

3.2 Analytics Cookies (Optional)

Google Analytics 4 is loaded only after you choose "Accept All". Before that decision, analytics and advertising consent are set to denied. If you decline, the site does not load GA4 and removes GA cookies that it can access.

  • Google Analytics 4 — After consent, GA4 records page and session activity, referral information, consent events, contact/download interactions, form attempts, and confirmed inquiry events. A confirmed inquiry event may include the inquiry type and requested product, but our code does not send your name, email address, company, country, free-text requirements, or attachment to GA4. Google Signals and advertising personalization are disabled. Analytics retention follows the current GA4 property configuration. Google Privacy Policy

3.3 Manage Your Cookie Preferences

You can change your preference at any time using the controls below.

Current status: checking...

You can also block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser. Clearing the consent record causes the preference banner to appear again.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share data only with:

  • Hosting and email providers: The PHP form handler validates the request and uses PHPMailer to send it through authenticated SMTP to the configured Begapunk sales mailbox. The handler does not write inquiry fields or attachments to a website database.
  • Analytics provider: Google Analytics receives the analytics data described above only after consent.
  • Shipping carriers: DHL, FedEx, or local carriers (only shipping address and contact info)
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described above and for applicable legal obligations:

  • Inquiry data: The current website handler does not create a database record. The email and any attachment remain in the sales mailbox according to operational, follow-up, warranty, and legal needs.
  • Uploaded files: PHP processes an attachment from temporary upload storage for the SMTP message; the website code does not intentionally retain a separate server copy after the request.
  • Rate-limit data: Recent attempt timestamps are evaluated within a 15-minute window in server temporary storage.
  • Order data: Retained as required by applicable tax, accounting, warranty, and commercial laws.
  • Analytics data: Retained according to the current Google Analytics property configuration.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are located in the European Union or California, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to processing or request restriction
  • Request data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at sales@begapunk.com.

7. Data Security

We use safeguards including TLS encryption, same-origin checks, rate limiting, a honeypot field, length and email validation, file-size/type/signature checks, authenticated SMTP, and restricted access to the receiving mailbox. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the effective date.

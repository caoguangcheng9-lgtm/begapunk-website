Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 11, 2026
Last Updated: June 11, 2026

Begapunk Precision Rotary Joint Manufacturer ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or submit an inquiry.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, company name, and country — provided voluntarily when you submit a quote request or contact form.
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and pages visited — collected automatically via cookies and analytics tools.
  • Business Information: Machine drawings (STEP, PDF, DWG) and technical specifications uploaded during the inquiry process.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide quotes
  • Process and fulfill orders
  • Verify product fit using uploaded technical drawings
  • Improve our website and product offerings
  • Send occasional product updates (only if you opt in)
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate and improve our website. Below is a breakdown of the cookies we use, their purpose, and how you can control them.

3.1 Essential Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be disabled. They do not store any personally identifiable information beyond your cookie preference.

  • Consent State — Stores your cookie preference (accept/decline) so the banner does not reappear on every visit. Expires after 365 days.

3.2 Analytics Cookies (Optional)

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website. They are only placed if you click "Accept All" on the cookie banner. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and no tracking data is sent.

  • Google Analytics 4 — We use GA4 to collect anonymized traffic data (page views, session duration, referral sources). GA4 is configured with advertising features disabled and IP anonymization enabled. Data is retained for 14 months. Google Privacy Policy

You can also disable cookies entirely through your browser settings. Note that essential cookies (consent state) are required for the banner to function correctly.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share data only with:

  • Service providers: FormBold (form processing), Google Analytics (website analytics)
  • Shipping carriers: DHL, FedEx, or local carriers (only shipping address and contact info)
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, typically:

  • Inquiry data: 3 years (for follow-up and warranty support)
  • Order data: 7 years (for tax and accounting compliance)
  • Analytics data: 26 months (Google Analytics default)

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are located in the European Union or California, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to processing or request restriction
  • Request data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at sales@begapunk.com.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including SSL encryption, access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the effective date.

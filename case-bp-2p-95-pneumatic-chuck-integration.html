The project owner confirmed the model as BP-2P-95-0001. Customer-authorized workshop photographs show its visible position in a pneumatic chuck assembly and compressed-air routing from the stationary side toward rotating components; the photographs do not independently establish model identity.

Pneumatic chuck integration

Connection Detail

The close view is the primary installation evidence. It shows the rotary-union mounting area and two visible pneumatic hoses routed inside the chuck assembly.

Engineering purpose

The arrangement provides a compact connection between stationary air lines and rotating chuck components.

Before design release

  • Confirm the required pneumatic circuits and the ordered product configuration.
  • Check port orientation, hose bend radius and interference clearance.
  • Verify mounting dimensions against the approved drawing for the ordered unit.

Select the photograph to open the full-resolution JPG.

Assembly Overview

The overview photograph records the relationship between the chuck, drive section, fixed-side pneumatic lines and the rotary-union installation area during workshop assembly.

What is visible

  • The rotary union is located close to the chuck's axis.
  • The stationary-side hoses approach the union without rotating with the chuck.
  • The installation occupies a confined central area within the photographed assembly.

What still requires engineering confirmation

Pressure, rotational speed, duty cycle, final clearances and the exact ordered interface must be confirmed from the approved drawing and the machine requirements.

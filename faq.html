Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Begapunk rotary joints — from selection to installation.

? General Questions

A rotary joint (also called rotary union or swivel joint) is a precision mechanical device used to transfer fluid — air, water, oil, coolant, or steam — from a stationary source to a rotating destination, or vice versa. Begapunk manufactures single-channel and multi-channel rotary joints for industrial automation, CNC machining, laser cutting, packaging, and other rotating machinery applications.

There is no technical difference — the terms are used interchangeably in the industry. "Rotary union" is more common in North America, while "rotary joint" is widely used in Europe and Asia. Both refer to the same device: a mechanical seal assembly that transfers fluid between stationary and rotating components.

★ Product Selection

The key parameters are:

  • Media type: Air, water, coolant, hydraulic oil, steam, or other
  • Number of passages: 1-in-1-out, 2-in-2-out, 3-in-3-out, or custom multi-channel
  • Max pressure: In bar or psi
  • Max speed: In RPM
  • Connection type: Threaded (NPT/BSP) or flange mount
  • Mounting space constraints: Any dimensional limitations

⚙ Installation & Maintenance

We offer two standard mounting types:

  • Threaded mount: NPT or BSP threads — connect directly to your equipment
  • Flange mount: Bolt-on flange with O-ring face seal — provides rigid, leak-free connection

Custom mounting configurations are available. Important: Always use flexible hose connections — never hard-pipe a rotary joint. See our Installation Guide for details.

The most common causes of premature failure are:

  • Hard piping: Rigid connections transfer vibration and misalignment to bearings and seals
  • Side-loading: Hoses under tension or sharp bends apply lateral force to the joint
  • Contamination: Unfiltered media grinds grooves into seal faces — use 25 micron or finer filtration
  • Over-speed / over-pressure: Exceeding rated specifications
  • Rigid anti-rotation: Anti-rotation devices must allow the joint to self-center

Following our Installation Guide will prevent most of these issues.

Yes. Most Begapunk rotary joints can be rebuilt using OEM seal kits. Use replacement seals that match the specified material, hardness, geometry, and surface requirements. Non-equivalent seals may cause premature wear or leakage. Contact our support team with your product serial number to order the correct seal kit.

Begapunk requires fluid filtration to 25 microns or finer for general applications, and 10 microns or finer for high-speed or precision rotary unions. Contaminants larger than the seal gap will embed in the seal material and cause irreversible shaft scoring. Inadequate filtration may void your warranty.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Begapunk rotary joints — from selection to installation.

? General Questions

A rotary joint (also called rotary union or swivel joint) is a precision mechanical device used to transfer fluid — air, water, oil, coolant, or steam — from a stationary source to a rotating destination, or vice versa. Begapunk manufactures single-passage and multi-passage rotary unions for industrial automation, CNC machining, laser cutting, packaging, and other rotating machinery applications.

There is no technical difference — the terms are used interchangeably in the industry. "Rotary union" is more common in North America, while "rotary joint" is widely used in Europe and Asia. Both refer to the same device: a mechanical seal assembly that transfers fluid between stationary and rotating components.

★ Product Selection

The key parameters are:

  • Media type: Air, water, coolant, hydraulic oil, steam, or other
  • Number of passages: 1-in-1-out, 2-in-2-out, 3-in-3-out, or custom multi-passage
  • Max pressure: In bar or psi
  • Max speed: In RPM
  • Connection type: Threaded (NPT/BSP) or flange mount
  • Mounting space constraints: Any dimensional limitations

A machine drawing is helpful but not required. Begapunk aims to respond promptly to complete technical inquiries. Response time depends on the application complexity and the information provided.

Compatible media are model-specific. Review the current product page and approved drawing; confirm wetted materials, seal compound, pressure, temperature, viscosity, cleaning chemistry, and filtration for the selected configuration.

Model ranges vary. Use the current product page and approved drawing for pressure, speed, temperature, medium, and mounting limits; requirements outside published limits need engineering review.

Select the passage count according to the number of independent medium circuits. A laser tube cutting machine rear chuck may require multiple independent compressed-air circuits. Confirm the circuit count and every port function against the chuck and complete machine design.

Body, shaft, and seal materials vary by model and configuration. Use the current product page and approved drawing; material substitutions or regulated and food-contact service require documented engineering review.

✉ Custom Orders & Delivery

Custom passage count, mounting, and materials can be reviewed. CAD files, drawing approval, inspection scope, price, and lead time are confirmed for the selected project and order.

Minimum order quantity is confirmed for the selected model, customization scope, and quotation or order.

Lead time and shipping terms depend on model, quantity, customization, destination, and the accepted quotation or order.

Warranty coverage and claim handling follow the accepted quotation, order, and applicable Terms; contact Begapunk with order and inspection details.

The current standard production leak check is performed after assembly at 1.0 MPa, with approximately 1 second of pressurization and 4 seconds of hold per passage. Any project-specific record or additional inspection must be agreed before order.

STEP/IGES files may be provided for qualified projects after model and application review; format and timing are confirmed per project.

⚙ Installation & Maintenance

Threaded and flange mounting are available on selected models. Confirm the exact interface on the current product page and approved drawing, and use flexible connections unless the approved installation specifies otherwise.

Potential contributors to early leakage include rigid piping, misalignment or side load, contamination, operation outside approved limits, and unsuitable anti-rotation. Review the approved installation and inspection procedure.

Repairability and seal-kit availability depend on the model and its condition. Contact Begapunk with the model, serial or order details, operating conditions, and inspection findings before repair.

Filtration requirements are model- and medium-specific. Confirm the required grade for the selected configuration; a clean supply helps reduce abrasive wear, but no universal micron value is claimed here.

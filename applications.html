Application-Driven Selection

Built Around Real Machine Requirements

Begapunk helps machine builders, OEM engineers, and distributors select air rotary unions based on passage count, pressure, RPM, mounting method, air quality, and installation environment.

Instead of choosing only by model number, engineers can start from the machine function: clamping, unclamping, blow-off, vacuum, indexing, rotating fixture supply, or protected pneumatic transfer in dusty environments.

Common selection inputs

  • Number of independent air passages
  • Maximum pressure and rotational speed
  • Mounting space, flange pattern, or thread type
  • Compressed air quality and filtration
  • Need for 2D drawing or 3D CAD review

Where Begapunk Air Rotary Unions Are Used

Start from your machine type, then match the passage count, mounting method, and protection level.

Automation Rotary Tables

Rotary indexing tables in automation lines need continuous air supply to pneumatic clamps, vacuum ejectors, and blow-off nozzles while rotating 360°. A failed rotary joint stops the entire indexing cycle and scrapes the production batch.

Typical requirement: 2–4 independent air passages at 0.3–0.6 MPa, 60–120 RPM indexing speed, with flange mount for anti-rotation stability. Multi-passage layouts prevent cross-contamination between clamp and vacuum circuits.

Typical air functions Clamping, unclamping, blow-off, vacuum holding, sensor air.
Recommended products BP-2P-0001 (2-passage flange), BP-3P-0004 (3-passage), BP-4P-30-0001 (4-passage, 30mm bore), BP-8P-0001 (8-passage high-channel).

Packaging Machinery

Rotary packaging machines — pouch fillers, form-fill-seal lines, and rotary sealers — need compact air transfer in tight machine envelopes. Space is limited, but downtime is expensive: a single failed seal can contaminate a full batch of food or pharmaceutical product.

Typical requirement: 1–3 passages at 0.4–0.8 MPa, compact threaded mount for tight turret envelopes, with quick-disconnect fittings for fast maintenance changeout. Stainless steel body options available for washdown environments.

Typical air functions Sealing head actuation, indexing control, vacuum release, film tension.
Recommended products BP-1P-0003 (compact single-passage), BP-2P-0001 (2-passage flange), BP-3P-0004 (3-passage compact), custom layouts for OEM integration.
View Application

Laser Tube Cutting Machines

Laser tube cutting heads rotate continuously while feeding assist gas (oxygen, nitrogen, or compressed air) and controlling pneumatic clamps. A leak in the assist gas line destroys cut quality; a clamp failure drops the workpiece into the machine.

Typical requirement: 2–4 passages at 0.4–1.0 MPa assist gas pressure, 100–500 RPM continuous rotation, with clean flow paths to prevent gas contamination. Flange mount preferred for high-vibration cutting heads.

Typical air functions Assist gas (O₂/N₂/air), pneumatic clamping, blow-off dust removal, coolant support.
Recommended products BP-2P-0001 (assist gas + clamp), BP-3P-0006 (3-passage G1/4), BP-4P-30-0001 (4-passage with bore), custom multi-passage for gas + coolant + clamp.
View Application

CNC Fixtures and Pneumatic Clamping

CNC rotary tables and 4th-axis fixtures rely on stable pneumatic clamping pressure to hold workpieces during machining. Pressure drop during rotation causes clamp release — and a destroyed workpiece or broken tool. Repeatable clamping force is non-negotiable.

Typical requirement: 2–3 passages at 0.3–0.6 MPa, flange mount for rigid fixture integration, with through-bore options for coolant or wiring passage. Derate pressure 30% for continuous-duty clamping cycles.

Typical air functions Fixture clamping, unclamping, pressure holding, rotary table air supply, coolant through-bore.
Recommended products BP-2P-0001 (clamp + unclamp), BP-3P-0004 (3-passage flange), BP-4P-30-0001 (4-passage with 30mm through-bore), custom for coolant integration.
View Application

Bottle Filling and Capping Machines

Rotary filling turrets and capping heads in beverage and pharmaceutical lines rotate at 60–200 RPM while controlling pneumatic torque, vacuum hold-down, and bottle gripper actuation. A leak at the rotary joint causes inconsistent fill levels or loose caps — both are rejected product.

Typical requirement: 1–3 passages at 0.2–0.5 MPa, compact threaded mount for tight turret envelopes, with FDA-compatible seal options for food-grade applications. Stainless steel 316 body available for washdown compliance.

Typical air functions Filling head actuation, capping torque control, vacuum bottle hold-down, gripper air, washdown blow-off.
Recommended products BP-1P-0003 (compact single-passage), BP-2P-0001 (2-passage), BP-3P-0004 (3-passage compact), custom FDA-grade configurations.
View Application

Robot End-of-Arm Tooling (EOAT)

Robot grippers, suction cups, and rotating wrists need air and vacuum transfer through a compact rotary joint at the end of the arm. Weight and size are critical — every gram at the EOAT reduces payload capacity. A failed joint means dropped parts and production line stops.

Typical requirement: 2–3 passages at 0.3–0.6 MPa, ultra-compact threaded mount (often M5 or G1/8), lightweight 6061 aluminum alloy body, with integrated electrical slip ring options for combined pneumatic-electric EOAT designs.

Typical air functions Gripper clamp/unclamp, vacuum suction, blow-off release, tool changer actuation, sensor air.
Recommended products BP-1P-0003 (ultra-compact), BP-2P-0002 (2-passage G1/8), BP-3P-S06-0001 (3-passage + 6 electrical circuits), custom EOAT layouts.
View Application

Pneumatic Tools and Hose Anti-Twist

Handheld pneumatic tools — tightening guns, grinders, sanders — twist the air hose with every rotation. Without a swivel, the hose kinks, restricts airflow, and fatigues at the fitting. A compact air swivel at the tool inlet eliminates hose twist and extends hose life by 3–5×.

Typical requirement: Single passage at 0.4–0.8 MPa, threaded mount (G1/4 or G1/8), compact body for handheld tool integration, with 360° free rotation and low torque drag so the operator does not feel resistance.

Typical air functions Tool inlet air supply, hose strain relief, anti-twist 360° rotation.
Recommended products BP-1P-0003 (G1/4, compact), BP-1P-0006 (1-in-6-out, G1/8, 4mm orifice), BP-2P-0002 (dual passage for tool + exhaust).
View Application

Vacuum Packaging Machines

Rotary vacuum packaging turrets need simultaneous vacuum extraction and release air on independent channels. Cross-contamination between vacuum and compressed air destroys package seal integrity. Each passage should be specified with an application-appropriate isolation and allowable cross-port leakage criterion. Confirm the channel-isolation test conditions and acceptance limits for the selected model.

Typical requirement: 2–3 passages (vacuum + release air + clamp), 0.05–0.1 MPa vacuum side / 0.4–0.6 MPa air side, 60–120 RPM turret speed, with flange mount for rigid turret integration and food-grade seal options.

Typical air functions Vacuum extraction, release air, clamp actuation, sealing bar air, pouch gripper vacuum.
Recommended products BP-2P-0001 (vacuum + air), BP-3P-0004 (3-passage), BP-3P-0006 (G1/4 multi-line), custom air/vacuum isolated layouts.
View Application

Welding Positioners and Pipe Rotators

Rotary welding tables and pipe rotators operate in harsh environments: weld spatter, grinding dust, and high vibration. Standard rotary joints fail within weeks from dust ingress and side-load damage. Protected designs with sealed bearings and dust shields are essential.

Typical requirement: 2–4 passages at 0.4–0.8 MPa, 5–60 RPM slow rotation, flange mount with IP65 dust protection, Grade 45 carbon steel or 304 SS body for weld spatter resistance, with heavy-duty anti-rotation bracket for vibration isolation.

Typical air functions Fixture clamping, unclamping, blow-off spatter removal, shielding gas support, positioner brake air.
Recommended products BP-2P-50-0001 (IP65 dust-proof), BP-3P-0004 (3-passage flange), BP-4P-30-0001 (4-passage heavy-duty), custom protected layouts with weld spatter shields.
View Application

Textile, Printing, and Converting

Rotating rollers, winding shafts, and printing drums need air blow-off, vacuum web handling, and sometimes coolant or water transfer. Paper dust and textile fibers are abrasive contaminants that score standard seals. Filtration and seal material selection are critical for 24/7 converting lines.

Typical requirement: 1–2 passages at 0.2–0.5 MPa, 100–500 RPM continuous rotation, through-bore for shaft passage, with 10-micron filtration and PTFE seals for fiber resistance. Water-compatible stainless steel body for coolant applications.

Typical air functions Web blow-off, vacuum web holding, roller cooling, tension control air, edge trim vacuum.
Recommended products BP-1P-0003 (blow-off), BP-2P-0001 (air + vacuum), BP-2P-130-0001 (SS body, water/coolant compatible), custom through-bore for shaft integration.
View Application

Electronics and Battery Test Fixtures

Rotating test fixtures for batteries, PCBs, and semiconductor components need precise pneumatic clamping and vacuum holding during inspection. ESD-sensitive environments require clean, dry air with no oil contamination. Compact size is critical — test fixtures often have <50 mm clearance around the rotation axis.

Typical requirement: 2–3 passages at 0.1–0.4 MPa, 60–300 RPM, ultra-compact threaded mount (M5 or G1/8), with oil-free PTFE seals and optional integrated electrical circuits for signal transfer alongside pneumatic lines.

Typical air functions Test fixture clamping, vacuum component holding, blow-off debris removal, probe actuation, ESD-safe air supply.
Recommended products BP-1P-0003 (ultra-compact), BP-2P-0002 (G1/8 dual), BP-3P-S06-0001 (3-passage + 6 electrical circuits), custom pneumatic-electric integrated designs.
View Application

Steel Mills and Dusty Industrial Environments

Steel mills, foundries, cement plants, and mining equipment expose rotary joints to abrasive dust, metal chips, and extreme vibration. Standard joints fail within 2–3 months from dust ingress into bearings and seal faces. IP65-rated dust-proof designs with labyrinth seals and protective boots are mandatory.

Typical requirement: 2–4 passages at 0.4–1.0 MPa, 10–100 RPM, flange mount with IP65 dust protection, Grade 45 carbon steel or 304 SS body for impact resistance, with sealed bearings and external dust shields. 25-micron filtration minimum; 10-micron recommended.

Typical air functions Pneumatic clamping in dusty environments, blow-off debris removal, actuator control, conveyor brake air, protected tool air supply.
Recommended products BP-2P-50-0001 (IP65 dust-proof, 2-passage), BP-3P-0004 (3-passage flange), BP-4P-30-0001 (heavy-duty 4-passage), custom protected layouts with dust boots and sealed bearings.

Selection Parameters

How to Select an Air Rotary Union for Your Application

The best model depends on machine function, installation constraints, and operating conditions.

Number of passagesChoose independent air channels for each machine function.
MediaCompressed air, vacuum, or air plus coolant requirements.
Pressure and RPMConfirm continuous operating pressure and rotational speed.
Mounting typeThreaded, flange, through-bore, or custom mounting.
Hose routingPrevent side loading and protect the anti-rotation support.
Air qualityUse filtration to protect the seal and reduce early leakage.
Material and sealsMatch body and seal options to the environment.
Drawing or CADReview 2D drawings or request 3D files before production.

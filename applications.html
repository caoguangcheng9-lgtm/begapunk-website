Documented application category

Laser Tube Cutting Rear Chucks

Laser tube cutting machine rear-chuck compressed-air transfer is documented for BP-3P-0004 and BP-2P-08-0001. Circuit count and interfaces must be confirmed from the chuck and machine design.

Process or assist-gas transfer is handled as a separate engineering project with application-specific materials, cleaning, testing, and approval.

Standard models used for rear-chuck compressed air: BP-3P-0004 and BP-2P-08-0001 for laser tube cutting machine applications.

Customer-authorized project

CNC Pneumatic Clamping Fixture

BP-2P-130-0001 is documented at the rear of a custom CNC machining fixture on a circular-blade saw machine. Two independent compressed-air passages perform fixture clamp and release.

For similar equipment, port arrangement, pressure, rotational speed, duty cycle, and mounting interface require review against the fixture drawing.

Bottle-Capping Head

BP-2P-16-0001 is documented in a production capping machine. Two independent passages carry compressed air for clamping and releasing the pneumatic three-jaw gripper.

Final materials, cleaning conditions, applicable requirements, and supporting documentation are established through configuration review.

Engineering Inputs

What to send for an application review

A machine name alone is not enough. These inputs allow Begapunk to compare a standard model or define a custom configuration.

  1. 1
    Medium and function

    Identify what each rotating passage carries and what the circuit does.

  2. 2
    Passage count

    List the functions that must remain independently controlled.

  3. 3
    Pressure and speed

    Provide the maximum pressure and rotational speed for the machine.

  4. 4
    Mounting interface

    Share the available envelope, thread or flange, and connection direction.

  5. 5
    Drawing context

    Attach a 2D drawing, STEP assembly, or marked-up installation sketch when available.

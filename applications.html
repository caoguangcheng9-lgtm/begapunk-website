Application-Driven Selection

Built Around Real Machine Requirements

Begapunk helps machine builders, OEM engineers, and distributors select air rotary unions based on passage count, pressure, RPM, mounting method, air quality, and installation environment.

Instead of choosing only by model number, engineers can start from the machine function: clamping, unclamping, blow-off, vacuum, indexing, rotating fixture supply, or protected pneumatic transfer in dusty environments.

Common selection inputs

  • Number of independent air passages
  • Maximum pressure and rotation speed
  • Mounting space, flange pattern, or thread type
  • Compressed air quality and filtration
  • Need for 2D drawing or 3D CAD review

Selection Parameters

How to Select an Air Rotary Union for Your Application

The best model depends on machine function, installation constraints, and operating conditions.

Number of passagesChoose independent air channels for each machine function.
MediaCompressed air, vacuum, or air plus coolant requirements.
Pressure and RPMConfirm continuous operating pressure and rotation speed.
Mounting typeThreaded, flange, through-bore, or custom mounting.
Hose routingPrevent side loading and protect the anti-rotation support.
Air qualityUse filtration to protect the seal and reduce early leakage.
Material and sealsMatch body and seal options to the environment.
Drawing or CADReview 2D drawings or request 3D files before production.

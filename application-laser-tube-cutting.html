Rear-Chuck Compressed Air

Carry independent air functions into the rotating chuck

For: Laser tube cutting machine builders and engineers integrating the rear-chuck pneumatic system.

The rotary union connects the stationary air supply to independently controlled functions in the rotating rear chuck. The passage count follows the number of air functions that must remain separate.

BP-3P-0004 and BP-2P-08-0001 are standard starting points for this application. The workshop photographs show the installation type; the exact model in each image has not been identified.

FAQ

Rear-Chuck Pneumatic Rotary Union Questions

Which standard models are used for rear-chuck compressed air?

BP-2P-08-0001 provides two independent passages, and BP-3P-0004 provides three. Choose according to the number of separately controlled air functions and the required interface.

What do I need to send for a model recommendation?

A chuck model, photo, or drawing is enough to start. Add the circuit count, pressure, and speed if they are available.

How are process or assist gases handled?

They require a separate solution. Tell us the gas and operating conditions so we can check materials, cleanliness, and testing.

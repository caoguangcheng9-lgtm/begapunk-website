Pneumatic Rotary Unions for Laser Tube Cutting Rear Chucks

Engineering guidance for supplying independent compressed-air circuits from the stationary side to the rotating rear chuck.

Send Rear-Chuck SpecificationsView the Real Application
Verified Application Scope

Compressed-air transfer at the rotating rear chuck

For: Laser tube cutting machine builders and engineers integrating the rear-chuck pneumatic system.

The rotary union carries independent compressed-air circuits across the stationary-to-rotating interface near the rear chuck. The required circuit count and every operating condition must be taken from the chuck drawing and pneumatic schematic.

BP-3P-0004 and BP-2P-08-0001 are standard models used for independent compressed-air circuits at the rear chuck in this application category. The workshop photographs document the application category, but the product shown in each photograph has not been individually identified as either model.

Real installation categoryRear-chuck compressed-air rotary supply in a laser tube cutting machine.

Information required for selection

  • Number of independently controlled compressed-air circuits
  • Operating pressure for each circuit
  • Maximum and continuous rotational speed
  • Available installation envelope and surrounding chuck geometry
  • Connection dimensions and mounting interface
  • Required hose direction and maintenance access

Engineering Review

Rear-chuck selection checklist

1. Circuit definition

Count only the compressed-air circuits that must remain independently controlled. Document the final channel assignment from the machine pneumatic schematic.

2. Operating conditions

Provide operating pressure plus maximum and continuous rotational speed. The selected model must be checked against the approved product data for the actual duty cycle.

3. Mechanical integration

Review the chuck drawing, connection dimensions, mounting interface, available space, hose direction and maintenance access before confirming the order configuration.

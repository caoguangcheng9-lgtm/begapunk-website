From requirement to shipment

Inquiry to Delivery Process

A clear engineering workflow for standard replacements and custom rotary-joint projects. The exact sequence may vary depending on application complexity and project requirements.

  1. Step 01Customer Input

    Send Your Inquiry

    Share the model number or provide your operating media, pressure, rotational speed, number of passages, mounting details, drawing, photo, or reference sample.

    Output: Initial project requirements
  2. Step 02Engineering Review

    Engineering Review

    We review the operating media, pressure, RPM, temperature, number of passages, connection type, duty cycle, installation space, and available technical information.

    Output: Feasibility and missing-information review
  3. Step 03Selection Route

    Standard Model or Custom Evaluation

    Where possible, we recommend a suitable standard model. If no direct match is available, the requirement moves to custom engineering evaluation.

    Output: Recommended model or custom route
  4. Step 04Technical Confirmation

    Drawing Confirmation

    Interfaces, port layout, passages, dimensions, mounting arrangement, and applicable operating conditions are confirmed before order execution.

    Output: Confirmed technical configuration
  5. Step 05Commercial Offer

    Quotation

    We issue a quotation based on the confirmed configuration, quantity, commercial terms, and estimated project schedule.

    Output: Formal commercial offer
  6. Step 06Order Release

    Order Confirmation

    After the purchase order and agreed payment terms are confirmed, the order is released for production or preparation.

    Output: Approved order
  7. Step 07Execution

    Production & Inspection

    Products are manufactured or prepared, assembled where applicable, and checked against the approved drawing and order requirements.

    Output: Completed and checked product
  8. Step 08Delivery

    Shipment & Delivery

    The order is packed and shipped using the agreed method. Tracking or shipping documents are provided where applicable.

    Output: Customer receives the order

Process note: This is a typical workflow. Standard replacement orders may move through the process faster, while complex custom projects may require additional technical review, drawing confirmation, or sample evaluation.

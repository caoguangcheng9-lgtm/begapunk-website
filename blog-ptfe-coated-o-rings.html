PTFE-Coated O-Rings for Rotary Union Seals

A surface-treatment option for the O-rings used with composite slipper seals, selected for specific process-media requirements.

Begapunk offers PTFE-coated O-rings as an optional configuration for suitable rotary union applications. The coating adds a protective surface layer to the elastomer. It is selected for the application, not supplied as standard on every model.

The application

Why add a PTFE coating?

In a composite slipper-seal assembly, the O-ring provides preload and elastic support. Selecting the seal therefore means considering both the slipper ring and the compatibility of its supporting O-ring with the working medium.

PTFE, or polytetrafluoroethylene, is resistant to a wide range of chemicals. A PTFE coating is one surface-treatment option to consider when reviewing an O-ring configuration for a particular process medium.

Coating inspection

A closer look at coating thickness

The supplier's inspection report records a blue PTFE-coated sample marked 25 × 2. Local coating thickness was measured by examining a cut section under a metallurgical microscope.

The specified thickness range for this sample was 5–15 µm. All three measurement points were within that range.

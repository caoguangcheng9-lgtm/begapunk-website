Installation Guide

1

Verify Thread Compatibility

Confirm your equipment's mating thread matches the rotary joint thread specification. Download the 2D drawing for exact dimensions.

2

Apply Thread Sealant

Use PTFE tape or liquid thread sealant on the male threads. Do not overtighten — hand-tighten plus 1/4 turn maximum.

3

Secure Anti-Rotation

If your model includes an anti-rotation tab, secure it to a fixed point. Never let the body rotate freely with the shaft.

4

Connect Supply Lines

Use flexible hoses to avoid side-loading the rotary joint. Install a filter (40 micron or finer) upstream to protect the seal.

5

Run-In Procedure

Run at low pressure (0.2 MPa) and low speed for 5 minutes without load. Check for leaks, then gradually increase to operating conditions.

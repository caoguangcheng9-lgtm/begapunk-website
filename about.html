About Begapunk

Precision rotary joint manufacturing since 2010. Made in Ningbo, delivered worldwide.

Who We Are

Begapunk is a specialized rotary joint (rotary union) manufacturer based in Ningbo, Zhejiang Province, China. We design and produce single-channel and multi-channel rotary joints for industrial automation, CNC machining, laser cutting, plastic extrusion, wind energy, and other rotating machinery applications.

Unlike general-purpose hydraulic component suppliers, we focus exclusively on rotary joints. This specialization allows us to offer deeper engineering support, faster custom prototyping, and more competitive pricing than multi-product competitors like Deublin or Kadant for equivalent specifications.

Our factory covers 3,200 square meters with 28 CNC machines, 6 seal testing stations, and a dedicated R&D lab. Every rotary joint undergoes 100% pressure testing at 1.5× rated pressure before shipment.

Manufacturing Process

How We Make a Rotary Joint

1

CNC Machining

304/316 SS body machined on 5-axis CNC. Tolerance ±0.01mm.

2

Surface Treatment

Passivation and polishing for corrosion resistance.

3

Seal Assembly

PTFE, graphite, or ceramic seals installed in clean room.

4

Pressure Test

Pressure and leakage checks are specified by model. Confirm the applicable test pressure, duration, and acceptance criteria with Begapunk before ordering.

5

Pack & Ship

Rust-proof packaging with 3D CAD file and manual.

Our Journey

Company Timeline

2010

Founded in Ningbo

Started as a CNC machining workshop serving local automation companies. First rotary joint prototype completed.

2014

ISO 9001 Certification

Formalized quality management system. Introduced 100% pressure testing protocol. First export order to Germany.

2018

Custom Design Division

Launched dedicated custom engineering team. Delivered first multi-channel (6-passage) rotary joint for wind turbine application.

2023

200,000 Units Milestone

Cumulative production exceeded 200,000 units. Active customers in 40+ countries. Free 3D file program launched.

2026

Global Expansion

Website launched with full English technical documentation. DDP shipping to EU and North America. New 1,200m2 assembly hall added.

About Begapunk

Precision rotary joint manufacturing in Ningbo, China.

Who We Are

Begapunk is a Ningbo-based manufacturer of pneumatic rotary joints for industrial automation and rotating machinery. Established in 2022, we develop standard and custom single-passage and multi-passage solutions for transferring compressed air between stationary and rotating machine components.

Our team works with machine builders to review passage count, pressure, speed, mounting space, materials, and connection requirements before production. This helps align each standard model or custom configuration with the actual machine layout and operating conditions.

Components are machined to the approved drawing, assembled, and inspected in Ningbo. Each finished rotary joint is leak-tested passage by passage before packing. Any project-specific test or document requirements are agreed before production.

Manufacturing Process

How We Make a Rotary Joint

1

CNC Machining

Aluminum, carbon-steel, and stainless-steel components are machined to the approved drawing.

2

Surface Treatment

Anodizing, passivation, polishing, and other surface treatments are selected for the component material and order requirements.

3

Seal Assembly

PTFE, O-ring, graphite, or ceramic-based seal systems are assembled according to the selected model and operating medium.

4

Pressure Test

Each finished unit is leak-tested passage by passage. If your project requires a specific test record, tell us before ordering.

5

Pack & Ship

Products are packed for shipment with the agreed documents. Catalog model pages include a 2D drawing and a downloadable STEP AP214 file for a fit check.

Choosing a Rotary Union Manufacturer

How should I compare rotary union suppliers?

Compare suppliers against the same checklist: the medium, pressure, speed, passage count and port sizes the model is rated for; whether a model drawing and STEP file are available before ordering; how each unit is tested before shipment; lead time and minimum order quantity; the written warranty terms; and the documentation available for your destination market. Ask each supplier to state these in writing for the specific model you plan to use.

Begapunk is operated by Ningbo Begapunk Pneumatic Components Co., Ltd. in Ningbo, Zhejiang, China. What should I look for when choosing a rotary union supplier?

Assess four areas: engineering response, including drawings and application review; seal quality and service-life test evidence relevant to your operating conditions; stated lead times and sample support; and after-sales technical support with clear warranty terms. Ask each supplier for the evidence relevant to your machine. Begapunk provides custom interfaces, single-unit samples, passage-by-passage production testing and individual product traceability.

What does Begapunk test before shipment?

After final assembly, every finished Begapunk pneumatic rotary union and every passage is leak-tested with compressed air before packing and storage. Each passage is tested at 1.0 MPa while the remaining passages stay unpressurized and open. The test pressurizes for approximately 1 second, rotates the joint and holds pressure for approximately 4 seconds. A unit is released only after the instrument displays PASS for every passage.

What is the warranty policy for Begapunk rotary unions?

Begapunk provides a standard one-year warranty from the shipment date for defects in materials or workmanship, under the written order terms. Damage caused by incorrect installation, excessive pressure or speed, unsuitable or unfiltered air, and misuse is excluded from that coverage. Send the model, traceability number, operating conditions and photos of the problem for review. When a covered manufacturing defect is confirmed, the standard remedy is replacement at no charge.

Application Fit

Why electronics and battery fixtures need compact rotary transfer

Who this is for: Electronics automation engineers, battery equipment designers, and test fixture manufacturers building rotating inspection and handling stations.

Battery winding, cell handling, electronics testing, and rotary inspection stations often use air cylinders, vacuum cups, and grippers on rotating fixtures.

In these machines the rotary union must be compact, clean, and stable. If electrical signals are also required, the design should be reviewed as a pneumatic-electric combination rather than a simple air-only joint.

Typical requirementCompact air and vacuum transfer for rotating battery fixtures, electronics handling, test stations, and inspection equipment.

Typical Electronics & Battery Test Fixture Rotary Union Specs

  • Pressure: 0.2–0.5 MPa (vacuum/gripper) — low-pressure precision control
  • RPM: <100 (test stations) / up to 300 (winding machines)
  • Passages: 2–4 (air + vacuum + sensor air + blow-off)
  • Media: dry filtered air, vacuum — oil-free required for cleanroom
  • Mounting: compact threaded for tight fixture envelopes
  • Bore: 3–6 mm for small grippers; 6–10 mm for larger actuators
  • Material: anodized AL6061 or 304 SS for cleanroom and ESD compatibility
  • Integration: review pneumatic-electric combination when signals are required

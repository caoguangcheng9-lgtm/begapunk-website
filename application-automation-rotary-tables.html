Air Rotary Union for Automation Rotary Tables

Automation rotary tables and indexing stations require clean, repeatable pneumatic transfer for clamping, vacuum, blow-off, grippers, and actuator control. Begapunk provides standard and custom multi-passage air rotary unions for machine builders.

Application Fit

A central pneumatic interface for rotating automation stations

On a rotary indexing table, multiple fixtures may need air at different times. A rotary union allows the machine to supply pneumatic functions while keeping hoses organized and preventing twist damage.

The best model depends on passage count, station count, pressure, rotation speed, allowable leakage, and whether the rotary union must share space with wiring, sensors, or a slip ring.

Typical requirement2 to 8 passages for clamping, vacuum, blow-off, and separated station control.
Engineering Points

Automation Table Selection Checklist

Passage count

Map each pneumatic function before selecting the model. Adding one spare passage can simplify future tooling changes.

Indexing behavior

Confirm whether the table rotates continuously, indexes step by step, or stops at fixed dwell positions.

Combined utilities

Some systems need air plus electrical signals. In that case, a pneumatic-electric design or separate slip ring arrangement may be required.

FAQ

Automation Rotary Table Questions

Can one rotary union supply several stations?

Yes, if the passage layout is planned correctly. Each independent air function should have its own channel.

Can air and electrical signals be combined?

Yes. Begapunk has pneumatic-electric options, and custom designs can be reviewed when signal count and air passage count are known.
